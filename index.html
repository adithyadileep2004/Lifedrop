Why Is Blood Donation Important?

Blood donation is a voluntary act of giving blood to help people who need transfusions. Donated blood can help patients undergoing surgery, people injured in accidents, and individuals receiving treatment for certain medical conditions.

How Does Blood Donation Work?

01. Registration

Register your interest at an authorized blood donation centre.

02. Health Screening

Trained staff check your health and determine whether you are eligible to donate.

03. Donation

Qualified staff collect blood using sterile equipment.

04. Recovery

Follow the staff's advice, rest, and take refreshments before leaving.

Who Can Donate Blood?

Eligibility depends on age, weight, health, medical history, and local blood donation guidelines.

  • Meet the applicable age and weight requirements.
  • Be in suitable health on the day of donation.
  • Answer health screening questions honestly.
  • Follow the instructions of qualified donation staff.

Final eligibility is determined by the authorized blood donation centre.
